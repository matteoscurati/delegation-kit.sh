# Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-09-09._

This site uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, and only once the site operator
enables it. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking, no
advertising. You do not need to accept anything to read this site.

## Data controller

The site is operated by **Matteo Scurati**
([matteo.scurati@gmail.com](mailto:matteo.scurati@gmail.com)). For privacy
questions or to exercise your GDPR rights, write to that address.

## What is collected, and why

When you visit a page, a small script served by Cloudflare reports an
anonymous event with: the URL of the page, the referring URL if any, your
browser and operating system family, your country, a screen size category, and
page-performance timings (Core Web Vitals). Cloudflare Web Analytics does not
set cookies, does not fingerprint, and does not store IP addresses or any
individual user identifier; metrics are aggregated on Cloudflare's side.

The data is used to understand which docs are read and whether pages load
well, so they can be improved. **Legal basis:** legitimate interest under
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## Who processes it

**Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA), which also hosts this site on **Cloudflare Workers
(Static Assets)**, with EU edge locations serving EU traffic. Cloudflare's
processing is described in
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and
in its
[Web Analytics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/).
As infrastructure provider it also processes minimal request metadata (IP,
user agent, timestamp) for security and rate limiting.

## What this site does not do

No cookies. No fingerprinting. No cross-site or cross-device tracking. No
advertising networks or third-party trackers. No data is sold, rented, or
shared.

## External links

External links to GitHub, npm, and similar may set their own cookies once you
click through. Their privacy policies apply there.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you may access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to the
processing of your personal data, and lodge a complaint with the
[Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.gpdp.it/). Write to
the email above. Aggregated analytics cannot be linked back to you, so erasure
of a specific past visit is technically infeasible.

## Opt out

Use a content blocker that blocks `static.cloudflareinsights.com`, or do not
visit the site.

## Changes

If data collection changes, this page is updated and the date at the top
reflects the change.
